Hosting, Domains and SSL Management

Keep your digital assets fast, always reachable, and encrypted. We manage your corporate domain names, DNS routing, high-speed web hosting, and SSL security certificates end to end.

Service Overview

Zero-Friction Web Infrastructure & Security Governance

Expired domains, misconfigured DNS records, or lapsed SSL certificates can bring business operations and customer trust to an immediate halt.

Synoletics Solutions provides fully managed hosting, domain lifecycle custody, and SSL security administration for MSMEs, startups, and growing enterprises. We ensure your website, email records, and eCommerce portals stay fast, protected, and continuously available.

Key Features & Deliverables

Managed High-Speed Web Hosting

NVMe SSD-backed hosting environments tuned for corporate websites, custom portals, and eCommerce stores with proactive resource monitoring.

Domain Registration & DNS Governance

Domain acquisition, renewal tracking, registrar lock protection, and clean DNS configuration (A, CNAME, MX, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records).

SSL / TLS Encryption & HTTPS

Provisioning, installation, and automated renewal of DV, OV, and Wildcard SSL certificates with strict HSTS and modern cipher enforcement.

Automated Backups & Migration

Zero-downtime website migrations from legacy hosts, scheduled off-site backups, and rapid one-click restoration readiness.

Business Benefits & Strategic Value

  • Eliminate Accidental Downtime: Proactive renewal tracking prevents domain expiration or browser "Not Secure" SSL warnings.
  • Stronger Email Deliverability: Proper SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS setup protects your brand domain against spoofing and spam filters.
  • Better Search Rankings & Conversion: Fast server response times (TTFB) and verified HTTPS encryption directly improve SEO and buyer confidence.
