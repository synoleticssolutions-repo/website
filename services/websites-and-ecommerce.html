Websites and eCommerce Engineering

High-performance corporate websites, custom B2B/B2C online stores, and our flagship Webettan Micro-ECOM SaaS platform engineered to help MSMEs sell online at nominal charges that protect their revenue.

Service Overview

Digital Storefronts & Corporate Platforms Built for Conversion

Your website or online store is the primary operational touchpoint for modern buyers. At Synoletics Solutions, we design and engineer fast, accessible, SEO-structured web platforms tailored to your business model.

Whether you are a startup launching a new brand identity, an established enterprise modernizing legacy portals, or an MSME looking to sell products online without paying heavy marketplace commissions, our engineering team delivers secure, responsive, and easily maintainable solutions.

Featured Product Highlight

Webettan — Micro ECOM SaaS Platform for MSMEs

Many small and medium businesses struggle with high custom eCommerce development costs or steep third-party marketplace commissions. Webettan is our purpose-built Micro ECOM SaaS platform that helps MSMEs run their own branded website and online store at nominal charges that do not affect their revenue.

  • Predictable, nominal pricing with zero revenue-eating commissions
  • Clean product catalog, shopping cart, and direct order management
  • Mobile-first responsive checkout optimized for regional and global customers

Key Features & Deliverables

Custom Corporate Websites

Bespoke UI/UX design, semantic HTML5/CSS3 architecture, fast page speeds, and clear conversion journeys that establish brand authority.

Full-Scale eCommerce Stores

Custom product catalogs, inventory workflows, secure payment gateway readiness, tax/shipping rules, and customer account portals.

Technical SEO & Schema Foundation

Built-in Open Graph tags, Schema.org structured data, clean URL hierarchy, XML sitemaps, and Core Web Vitals optimization.

Responsive & Accessible UX

Fluid layouts across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices with WCAG-aligned contrast, keyboard navigation, and intuitive menus.

Business Benefits & Strategic Value

  • Protect Profit Margins: Own your customer relationships and direct orders without surrendering 15–30% per order to aggregator platforms.
  • Higher Search Visibility: Semantic markup and fast loading speeds help prospective B2B and B2C buyers find you organically.
  • Seamless Infrastructure Integration: Paired directly with our Hosting, Domains, SSL, and Cloud DevOps teams for single-point accountability.
