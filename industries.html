Sector Expertise

Empowering Both IT and Non-IT Sectors

Synoletics Solutions is uniquely structured to serve both digital-native IT businesses and traditional non-IT industries—delivering tailored solutions for MSMEs, Startups, and Existing Businesses ready to scale.

Target Audience Focus

Tailored by Business Stage

Every organization faces distinct technological and operational hurdles depending on its current stage of growth.

Segment 01

MSMEs (Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises)

MSMEs form the backbone of the economy but often face tight budgets and founder-dependent operations. We help MSMEs launch affordable online stores via Webettan and standardize daily work using clear checklists and SOPs.

  • Webettan Micro-ECOM SaaS at nominal charges
  • Managed domain, hosting & SSL care
  • Practical SOPs, daily checklists & staff training
Segment 02

Startups & Emerging Ventures

Startups need speed, technical scalability, and structured business plans to validate product-market fit and win investor or customer confidence without accumulating technical debt.

  • Business plans, feasibility & requirement analysis
  • Custom web/SaaS MVP engineering
  • Cloud server provisioning & automated CI/CD DevOps
Segment 03

Existing Businesses Scaling Up

Established companies expanding across new branches or regions require standardized quality management, internal audits, and modernized IT infrastructure to prevent operational chaos during growth.

  • Operations reviews & cross-department workflow mapping
  • Internal process audits & audit-readiness action plans
  • Enterprise web portals & high-availability cloud migration
Dual-Industry Capability

IT & Non-IT Industry Verticals & Use Cases

Below are representative sector use cases illustrating how our six services solve real-world challenges across both IT and Non-IT domains.

Industry Domain 01

IT, Software & Digital Businesses

Supporting SaaS startups, digital agencies, FinTech/EdTech ventures, and IT service providers with cloud reliability, release automation, and delivery governance.

Key Use Cases:

  • Cloud & DevOps Modernization: Automating zero-downtime CI/CD deployments and hardening Linux production servers.
  • IT Audit & Security Readiness: Conducting internal process audits, access-control reviews, and incident-response SOP authoring.
  • Product & Requirement Analysis: Translating founder vision into structured functional specifications and workflow maps.
Industry Domain 02

Non-IT, Retail, Manufacturing & Service Sectors

Empowering brick-and-mortar retail stores, wholesale distributors, manufacturing units, hospitality, healthcare clinics, and professional service firms.

Key Use Cases:

  • Direct-to-Consumer eCommerce: Onboarding retail and trading MSMEs onto Webettan to capture online orders at nominal costs.
  • Shop-Floor & Branch SOPs: Creating procurement, inventory, quality inspection, and customer service checklists with hands-on staff training.
  • Operational Streamlining: Mapping order-to-cash workflows and eliminating bottlenecks across multi-location operations.
