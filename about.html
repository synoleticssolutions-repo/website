Corporate Overview

About Synoletics Solutions

Synoletics Solutions is a forward-thinking IT and non-IT products and services provider committed to empowering businesses through technology, innovation, and strategic business solutions.

Founded in 2026, we specialize in Web Development, eCommerce Solutions, Business Consulting, Operations Management, and Project Management Services. We partner with startups, small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), and growing businesses to simplify complex challenges, enhance operational efficiency, and accelerate their digital transformation journeys.

Powered by a skilled and certified team of engineers, consultants, and strategists, we bring together technical expertise and business acumen to deliver secure, scalable, reliable, and cost-effective solutions tailored to our clients' unique requirements.

At Synoletics Solutions, we believe that successful business transformation goes beyond technology. It requires the right strategy, efficient processes, and innovative thinking. Our commitment is to build lasting partnerships, create measurable business value, and help organizations navigate change with confidence.

Company History & Milestones

Our Journey & Strategic Evolution

Established in 2026 to address a critical gap in the market: startups and SMEs needed a single trusted partner capable of delivering both reliable IT infrastructure and practical operational governance.

2026 — Foundation

Establishment in Kochi, Kerala

Synoletics Solutions was founded in 2026 with headquarters in Kochi and operational reach across Trivandrum, Kochi, and Thrissur, uniting certified software engineers, cloud architects, and business operations consultants.

2026 — Product Innovation

Launch of Webettan Micro-ECOM SaaS

Introduced Webettan, a dedicated Micro ECOM SaaS platform enabling MSMEs to establish and run full-featured online stores at nominal charges that protect their top-line revenue.

2026 — Integrated Practice

Six-Pillar IT & Non-IT Practice Rollout

Structured our service architecture into six cohesive pillars: Websites & eCommerce, Hosting/Domains/SSL, Servers/Cloud/DevOps, Business Plans & Analysis, Processes/Quality/Audit Readiness, and SOPs/Checklists/Training.

Ongoing — Global Delivery

Empowering MSMEs & Scaling Businesses Worldwide

Expanding remote and on-ground consulting engagements for startups, MSMEs, and scaling enterprises seeking end-to-end digital transformation and operational excellence.
